# Business Operations & Process Improvement

## Executive summary

A practical operations portfolio built around PhillyScreen's order-to-fulfillment system, with reusable SOP, KPI, and process-review tools for small and growing organizations.

## Business problem

Custom merchandise work combines sales promises, artwork, purchasing, production capacity, quality control, and delivery deadlines. When those steps live in informal messages, the business becomes vulnerable to rework, rush charges, missed dates, and unclear accountability.

## Scope

- Order intake and estimating
- Procurement and production scheduling
- Quality assurance and fulfillment
- Customer communication
- KPI tracking and process review

## Role and evidence basis

**Real case basis:** Founder & Owner, PhillyScreen LLC. Timothy managed end-to-end business operations including quoting, product pricing, supplier procurement, contract terms, production schedules, delivery, Shopify orders, inventory, purchase orders, costing, and forecasting.

**Demonstration status: Illustrative demonstration.** The SOP and KPI files are newly created illustrative tools. The PhillyScreen case write-up is limited to documented business facts.

## Methods and tools

Process mapping, standard work, checklist design, KPI definitions, capacity planning, vendor coordination, Shopify workflows, Excel tracking, and after-action review.
