# PhillyScreen Operations Case Write-Up

## Context

PhillyScreen LLC is a Philadelphia custom apparel, screen-printing, embroidery, merchandise, and promotional-products business founded and operated by Timothy Nesmith beginning in 2012.

## Operating challenge

Each order can involve a different product, decoration method, deadline, supplier, approval path, and delivery requirement. The owner must protect margin and quality while coordinating sales, artwork readiness, purchasing, production, and customer communication.

## Operating system

1. **Intake and estimate:** confirm quantity, product, decoration, deadline, delivery method, and approval owner.
2. **Costing:** calculate blanks, decoration, setup, freight, labor, and margin before quoting.
3. **Procurement:** issue purchase requirements early enough to protect the production date.
4. **Production control:** schedule by due date and capacity; flag artwork or inventory blockers daily.
5. **Quality check:** compare finished goods with the approved specification before packing.
6. **Fulfillment and closeout:** confirm delivery, invoice promptly, and record exceptions for review.

## Documented scale

- 500+ repeat customers
- 16 university clients and 30+ school clients
- Annual revenue of approximately $120,000–$400,000 during active full-time years
- Individual projects exceeding $100,000, per the master resume
- Shopify e-commerce combined with institutional and B2B production

## Transferable lesson

The same control points apply to many service businesses: a clear intake standard, visible queue, early blocker escalation, quality gate, and closeout review.
