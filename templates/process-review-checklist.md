# Process Review Checklist

**Process:** [Name]  
**Reviewer:** [Role]  
**Date:** [Date]

- [ ] Process purpose is written in one sentence.
- [ ] Process owner and backup are named.
- [ ] Inputs and required information are defined.
- [ ] Steps occur in the correct order with no undocumented handoffs.
- [ ] Approval points and exception authority are clear.
- [ ] Cycle-time and quality measures are current.
- [ ] Top three failure modes are recorded.
- [ ] Training material matches the current SOP version.
- [ ] Customer-facing commitments match operational capacity.
- [ ] Improvement actions have owners and due dates.
